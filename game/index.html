EMBER CLASH

A duel card battler. You against the Cinder Warden — 30 HP each, 26 cards, four elements, one deck.

HOW TO PLAY
· Each turn you get 3 energy. Cards cost energy — click one to play it.
· Element Echo: play two cards of the same element in one turn and the second one hits +2.
· Cinder shows its plan before you end your turn — answer it with block, healing, or a faster kill.
· Block fades at the start of your next turn. First to 0 HP loses.
· Keyboard: 1–7 play a card, Enter ends the turn.
Original game · no sign-up · runs fully offline

VICTORY

The Cinder Warden's embers fade. The table is yours.

DEFEAT

The Cinder Warden's embers burn on. Shuffle up and go again.
